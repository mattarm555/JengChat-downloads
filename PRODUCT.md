# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
The owner's friends, who get the link from the owner directly. They come to install JengChat on a Windows PC, or to check what the newest version brings. Most have played with the owner before. Some have the 2.x (Raylib) client installed already, and it updates itself.

## Product Purpose
This is the download page and update feed for JengChat. JengChat is a desktop app where a group of friends chat and play together: Chess, Blackjack, Poker, Roulette, JENG ARENA tanks, a 3D Lounge, chess puzzles, and JengGPT, a chat AI. The page succeeds when a friend gets the verified installer and opens the app. It is also the GitHub Pages site that serves the `/updates/` feed installed copies read.

## Positioning
It's a private hangout one person built for their friends, not a commercial product. Everything is in one app on one shared server: the 3D Lounge, where you walk up to a table to play; tables with dealers; chess at a 3D table with clocks; and an AI that lives in the chat.

## Operating Context
- The owner sends the link to friends. The page stays no-index, and its framing is "friends testing".
- Releases are manual. The owner runs the draft, pilot and publish workflows, and publishing writes `release.json`, which the page reads for the version, installer URL, SHA-256 and notes.
- Builds are unsigned, so Windows may warn about an unknown publisher.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Windows 10/11 64-bit only. Don't mention a Mac version.
- The site is static, with no third-party requests (no fonts, analytics or CDNs). `app.js` accepts installer and release links only from `github.com/mattarm555/JengChat-downloads/releases/`.
- The `/updates/` and `/pilot/` feed files are generated by `tools/publish_delivery.py` and must keep working.
- 3.0.0-beta.1 is the first Godot release. Its notes are in the JengChat repository at `releases/3.0.0-beta.1.md`.

## Brand Commitments
- The name is "JengChat", with the wordmark "JENG" in red and "CHAT" in gold.
- The app's palette is dark, with red `#e76c60` and gold `#e7be65` accents, and the app uses the Inter font.
- The owner asked for "a cool little UI design" that's mostly for friends.

## Evidence on Hand
- Real app screenshots come only from demo or fixture runs with made-up names (`--lounge-shot --lounge-demo`, `--chess-shot`, the casino presentation harnesses).
- There are no testimonials, user counts, press or ratings, and none should be invented.

## Product Principles
- It should feel like an invitation from a friend, not a sales pitch.
- Show the real app, not claims about it.
- Be honest about the install: unsigned, Windows only, checksum provided, and updates that ask first.
- Downloading should take one obvious click, and existing users should learn they don't need to reinstall.
